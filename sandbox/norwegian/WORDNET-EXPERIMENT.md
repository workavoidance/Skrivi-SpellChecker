# Optional offline word help

Run Setup-Wordnet-Experiment.cmd from the sandbox root once, then Start-Wordnet-Experiment.cmd. Existing assets are reused from the persistent cache; launch does not download anything. WORDNET-NOTICE.md records the source and attribution.

This experiment adds semantic clues and related senses where available. It does not guarantee a definition for every suggestion and does not replace the writer's choice. Historical coverage reports remain in the private recovery snapshot; no new coverage claim is made during migration.
