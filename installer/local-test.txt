Skrivi Rett 0.1.0 - local test build

Norwegian Bokmal only. This build reuses the existing Norwegian sandbox runtime,
models and dictionary cache on this PC. It does not download or bundle those assets.

The app installs separately from Skrivi Snakk, Lytt and the spelling sandbox.
Updating or uninstalling it leaves the shared model cache and personal word list intact.

This test installer is unsigned. Store packaging, signing and clean-PC setup are
not part of this build. Dictionary redistribution terms remain under review.
